# The reduction screen, replaced

Date: 2026-09-06
Status: **instrument replacement; no recorded result altered, no verdict revisited**

`so/screen.py`. This closes the three defects NOV-001 found and adds a guard for the one the old
predicate had no way to express. It is the second of the three actions
`frontier-update-nov002-2026-09-06.md` lists.

## What is deliberately *not* done

**E-000102 … E-000108 keep the predicate that produced their records.** Nothing in this change
touches their code, their results, or their verdicts. Rewriting an instrument underneath a recorded
result is how a ledger stops meaning anything, and this programme has already paid three times for
instruments that were not what they were believed to be. The old predicate stays where it is, with
§31.42 and §31.44 attached to it saying what it was.

New work imports from `so.screen`.

## The four changes

**1. Improvement, not equality.** The old predicate asked whether two cost figures *differ*. A
candidate spending strictly more arithmetic for the same states escaped the kill — NOV-001's M5, 32
multiplies against 16, better at nothing, verdict `PROMOTE`. `screen()` promotes only on a strict
improvement in at least one coordinate; a tie and a regression both return `KILL`.

**2. Every coordinate the mechanism moves.** The old predicate read multiplies alone, so a mechanism
that tied on arithmetic and moved strictly less memory was refused — NOV-001's M1, 36 words against
60. A `Measurement` declares which coordinates its comparison is entitled to read, and anything
undeclared is reported in `unread_coordinates` rather than silently scored as "no difference". That
matters more than it sounds: NOV-002 could not test M1 and M2 against E-000104 or E-000105 precisely
because neither original instruments memory traffic or sequential depth, and under the old predicate
there was nowhere for that absence to show up.

**3. The representation charged to whoever builds it.** The old convention handed the generic
baseline the candidate's own representation, so any mechanism whose advantage *is* its representation
tied by construction — NOV-001's M4, where the accounting alone flipped the verdict.
`CostVector.charged()` folds `representation_multiplies` into the total.

NOV-002 is the reason this is stated as a defect rather than a correction: charging did **not**
reverse E-000104 or E-000105. In E-000104 both arms pay the same build (ratio 1.00) and the tie is
real; in E-000105 charging makes the candidate strictly worse (ratio 3.04), exposing a regression the
old accounting was concealing in the candidate's favour. The subsidy was real and was not what
produced those two verdicts. Both facts belong in the record.

**4. A comparison whose two sides share a provenance label is refused.** This is the new one.

```
e000104:224   c_work = local_update_multiplies(rank)
e000104:225   g_work = local_update_multiplies(rank)
e000105:176   candidate_work = mutation_multiplies(net)
e000105:177   generic_work   = mutation_multiplies(net)
```

One pure function, called twice on one argument. Neither `work_mismatch` nor the
`total_candidate == total_generic` term could take any value but zero/equal, so both kills rested on
exact-state equality alone while reporting a work comparison they had not made.

A `Measurement` must name where its number came from, and `screen()` raises `VacuousComparison` when
both arms carry the same label. The defect becomes unrepresentable rather than remembered. Ledger
§31.15 — *an instrument that cannot fail is not evidence* — enforced by the type instead of by
whoever reviews the diff next.

Note what the guard does **not** do: it refuses a shared *source*, not a tied *number*. Two arms
measured independently that happen to cost the same are a real tie and screen to `KILL` normally.
There is a test for exactly that, because a guard that refused genuine ties would be the same class
of error in the other direction.

## Verdicts

| verdict | meaning |
|---|---|
| `NOT_EXACT` | the arms do not compute the same thing; cost is not yet the question |
| `PROMOTE` | identical states, and a strict improvement on at least one declared coordinate |
| `KILL` | identical states, and no improvement anywhere — a tie or a regression |

Exactness is checked by the caller and passed in. It is the one thing the old predicate got right and
it is not second-guessed here.

## Coverage

14 focused regressions in `so/tests/test_screen.py`, one per defect plus the boundary cases: a shared
label raises, an independent tie does not, an undeclared coordinate is reported rather than scored, a
genuine trade promotes and records both sides, and the E-000105 charged shape kills with
`regressed: multiplies 937,728 vs 329,472` rather than promoting.

## Limits

Four coordinates is not a full cost model — communication volume, cache behaviour, energy and
numerical conditioning are absent, and `unread_coordinates` exists so that absence is visible in the
record rather than assumed away. The screen also cannot tell whether a declared coordinate was
*measured well*; it only refuses the case where it was not measured twice.
